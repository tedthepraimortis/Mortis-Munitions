# Mortis-Munitions

A weapon pack made to utilize some of the more underutilized rounds in HDBulletLib-Recasted.

## Requirements

- [Hideous Destructor](https://codeberg.org/mc776/HideousDestructor)
- [HDBulletLib-Recasted](https://github.com/HDest-Community/HDBulletLib-Recasted)
- [HDest-Core-Lib](https://github.com/HDest-Community/hdest-core-lib)

## Weapon Information

__Taronga__

- A 6mm SAW made for being able to shoot armored or fleshy opposition through walls and doors thanks to the 6mm Flechette rounds it fires.
- Known for its highly modifiable yet ergonomic design, allowing for the end user to use either lighter or heavier parts in its construction.
- Feeds from a belt-bag system, requiring no belt links to go with, though always will require some form of pouch to use for ammo management.
- Never adopted due to its high maintenance requirements and nature of bolt seizes when feeding rounds for too long, along with heat dissipation issues.

Loadout Code: trn | stock 0-2, barrel 0-2, bolt 0-2, gl 0/1  
Attachments with 3 variants are all Stock/Lightweight/Heavyweight for their respective values. E.g. "trn stock 1 barrel 1 bolt 1" will give you a light stocked, light barreled, light bolt'd Taronga.

__Junco__

- A 4.26mm UAC Pistol made for shooting babuins off of yourself in the middle of fights, or for pairing with a non-standard LMG/SAW/MMG to fend off larger, tougher foes with.
- One of the earliest P.M.I. creations, being effectively a hacked down ZM turned into a pistol.
- Generates heat quite quickly, along with lacking a semi-auto fire selector option.
- Can burn the user if it gets too hot that the rounds start cooking off due to lack of padding and protection in the grip to save on weight.
- Very useful in a pinch if you need something fleshy dead quickly.

Loadout Code: jnk | firemode - 0-1, auto/burst
Magazine: 425 | 4mm round, unreloadable due to DRM

__Currawong__

- A 10mm PDW made for high-value targets to carry around with them after an acquisition of P.M.I by Volt thanks to the success of the Taronga and Junco.
- The most recent of P.M.I.'s creation, being developed entirely after the acquisition by Volt of all company property.
- Due to both manufacturing tolerance issues and general negligance and rush, the bolt tends to seize up and stick inside of the chamber when shot too much.
- Some come with underbarrel 4-gauge Buckshot launchers once the Mars invasion started to take out high health enemies, such as the recently nicknamed 'Barons of Hell', though very few were ever used.

Loadout Code: cwn | noshot - 0/1, semi - 0/1, firemode - 0-2, dot - 0-5
Magazine: c30 | 10mm Round, unreloadable due to DRM
4-Gauge Shotgun Shells: 4gb

__Featherduster__

- A .066 Bore Handheld Shotgun made for dealing with various types of pest on various farms.
- Notably not magazine fed due to cost concerns of having to buy new magazines along with .066 Bore shotshells, which at the time were not very common to find.
- Most of these shotguns are hand made, some featuring suppressors pre-attached to increase user comfort when clearing out crop-eaters.

Loadout Code: 6ps | suppressor - 0/1, choke - 0-4
Clips: 6p1 | Ammo: 066, .066 Bore
